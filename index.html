How It Works

From your first call to a space you can use again

A clear process, explained before we start, so you always know what is happening in your property.

You call, we answer

A real member of our team takes your call at any hour. We gather the few details we need and dispatch — no call-centre queue, no waiting until morning.

On-site assessment

Our technicians assess the full extent of the contamination, including what has spread beneath and behind the visible damage, then walk you through the scope and the plan.

Containment & remediation

We isolate the affected area, remove unsalvageable material, and decontaminate every surface with professional-grade products and the correct dwell times.

Odor, verification & handover

We treat residual odor at the source, verify the area, remove all regulated waste, and document the work so the property can be safely occupied again.
