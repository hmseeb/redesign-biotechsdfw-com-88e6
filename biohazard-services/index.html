What makes this different

Why biohazard work cannot be handled by a cleaning service

Blood-borne pathogens, decomposition fluids, chemical agents and accumulated waste all present exposure risk, and much of the contamination is invisible by the time anyone notices it. The work requires protective equipment, containment, the right products with the right dwell times, and a compliant disposal chain — not a mop and a bottle of bleach.

Biohazard Cleanup

Full-scope cleanup, disinfection and safe disposal of biological hazards in homes, vehicles and commercial property.

Biological contamination is not a cleaning problem — it is a containment problem. Our technicians isolate the affected area, remove porous materials that cannot be salvaged, treat every surface with hospital-grade disinfectants, and package all contaminated material for regulated disposal. We document the scope from start to finish so property owners, landlords and insurers have a clear record of what was remediated.

  • Containment & air control
  • Hospital-grade disinfection
  • Regulated waste disposal
  • Verification & documentation

Blood Cleanup

Safe removal and decontamination of blood and blood-borne pathogens after an accident, injury or violent incident.

Blood carries pathogens that stay infectious long after a surface looks dry. We treat every blood incident as potentially infectious, working in full personal protective equipment to remove the visible contamination and then the invisible residue beneath it — subfloor, drywall cavities, grout lines and upholstery included.

  • Blood-borne pathogen protocols
  • Sub-surface inspection
  • Porous material removal
  • Final disinfection

Crime Scene Cleanup

Discreet remediation of a property after law enforcement releases the scene, returning the space to a safe condition.

Once investigators release a scene, the cleanup becomes the property owner's responsibility. We step in at that point. Our crews arrive in a low-profile manner, coordinate access with whoever is managing the property, and restore the affected area — including fingerprint powder, chemical residue and biological material — so the space can be occupied again.

  • Post-release scene remediation
  • Discreet, low-profile response
  • Fingerprint & chemical residue
  • Odor neutralisation

Death Cleanup

Compassionate cleanup after a death at home or at a place of business, handled with care for the family involved.

When someone dies at home, the family should not be the ones cleaning up. We handle that work, and we handle it gently. Our technicians explain what will happen before they begin, protect belongings wherever possible, and remediate the affected area completely so the home is safe for the people who have to return to it.

  • Family-first communication
  • Belongings protected where possible
  • Complete affected-area remediation

Suicide Cleanup

Sensitive, thorough remediation after a suicide, so loved ones never have to confront the aftermath themselves.

There is no routine version of this call. We respond quickly and quietly, keep communication with the family to the minimum they want, and take care of everything from biological decontamination to removing and replacing unsalvageable materials. Our priority is that nobody in the family ever has to see or handle the aftermath.

  • Rapid, private response
  • Full biological decontamination
  • Material removal & disposal
  • Odor and residue treatment

Decomposition Cleanup

Remediation of unattended death and decomposition, including deep structural contamination and lingering odor.

An unattended death produces contamination that travels far past the visible stain — into subflooring, joists, HVAC runs and the air itself. We trace the full extent of the migration, remove what cannot be decontaminated, treat what can be, and then run odor abatement until the structure is genuinely clear rather than merely masked.

  • Full contamination mapping
  • Subfloor & structural work
  • HVAC consideration
  • Industrial odor abatement

Hazmat Cleanup

Identification, containment and disposal of hazardous materials and chemical contamination on your property.

Hazardous material incidents demand the right protective equipment, the right neutralising agents and the right disposal chain. Our hazmat crews assess the material involved, contain the affected area to stop the spread, decontaminate surfaces and structures, and transfer waste into a compliant disposal stream.

  • Material identification
  • Containment & neutralisation
  • Compliant disposal chain
  • Clearance documentation

Bodily Fluids Cleanup

Decontamination of vomit, urine, blood and other bodily fluids in residential, commercial and vehicle settings.

Bodily fluids soak into whatever they touch and keep producing bacteria and odor until they are fully removed. We extract the contamination, pull back flooring and fixtures where the fluid has penetrated, disinfect to the full depth of the spill, and treat the air and surfaces so the space is genuinely sanitary.

  • Depth-of-penetration removal
  • Antimicrobial treatment
  • Flooring & fixture work
  • Odor elimination

Feces / Urine Cleanup

Cleanup of human and animal waste accumulation, including sewage backups and severe pet contamination.

Accumulated waste is one of the highest-risk cleanups we perform: ammonia vapour, airborne pathogens and insect activity all build quickly. Our technicians work in respiratory protection to remove the waste and all saturated material, disinfect the structure underneath, and run air and odor treatment until the property is habitable again.

  • Respiratory-protected crews
  • Saturated material removal
  • Structural disinfection
  • Ammonia odor treatment

Drug Remediation

Decontamination of properties affected by drug use, drug manufacturing residue and related chemical hazards.

Drug activity leaves chemical residue on walls, ceilings, ventilation and belongings long after the activity stops. We assess how far the residue has spread, remove contaminated materials, and clean and treat the remaining surfaces and systems so landlords, property managers and new occupants are not inheriting an exposure risk.

  • Residue spread assessment
  • Surface & HVAC treatment
  • Sharps and paraphernalia removal
