Our approach

Sorting, not stripping

Hoarding and clutter cleanups go wrong when a crew arrives and starts throwing things in a skip. We work at a pace the client can tolerate, sort as we go, recover valuables, documents and keepsakes, and deal with the pest activity, waste and structural damage hidden beneath the volume. Where a property manager or agency is the client, we document each stage so there is a clear record of the work.

Hoarding Cleanup

Patient, judgement-free hoarding remediation that works with the client rather than simply hauling everything away.

Hoarding cleanups fail when they are rushed. We move at a pace the client can live with, sort rather than simply discard, and recover valuables, documents and keepsakes as we go. Underneath the clutter there is almost always biohazard and structural work to do, and we handle that too — pest contamination, waste, damaged flooring and blocked exits.

  • Judgement-free, paced approach
  • Sort, recover & document valuables
  • Biohazard & pest contamination
  • Safe egress restored

Clutter Cleanup

Large-scale clutter and debris removal for homes, estates, rentals and commercial spaces that have gotten away from you.

Not every overwhelmed space is a hoarding case. Estate clear-outs, long-vacant rentals, garages and storage units can simply need a crew, a plan and a truck. We sort what stays from what goes, remove the volume quickly, and leave the space clean and ready for its next use.

  • Whole-property clear-outs
  • Sorting & donation handling
  • Bulk debris removal
  • Clean, ready-to-use finish

Homeless Encampment Cleanup

Safe remediation of encampment sites for municipalities, property owners and commercial landholders.

Encampment sites combine bulk debris with genuine biohazard risk: sharps, human waste, spoiled food and accumulated trash. Our crews work the site in protective equipment, remove and dispose of the material under the correct waste streams, disinfect hard surfaces, and leave the property secure and sanitary.

  • Sharps & needle removal
  • Human waste decontamination
  • Bulk debris & structure removal
  • Site disinfection

Tear Gas Cleanup

Removal of CS and OC residue after a law-enforcement deployment, from surfaces, contents and ventilation.

Tear gas does not wash off. CS and OC agents settle as a fine, oily particulate that re-activates with heat and humidity, which is why a property can keep irritating occupants weeks after deployment. We treat the whole envelope — surfaces, soft contents, filters and ventilation — rather than just the room where the canister landed.

  • CS & OC particulate removal
  • Soft contents treatment
  • HVAC & filter remediation
  • Re-occupancy verification

Odor Treatment Service

Industrial odor elimination that removes the source rather than covering it with fragrance.

Odor is information: it tells you contamination is still present. We find and remove the source first, then use industrial treatment on the remaining structure and air until the odor is gone rather than masked. Decomposition, smoke, waste, pet and chemical odors are all within scope.

  • Source identification & removal
  • Industrial air treatment
  • Structural sealing where needed
  • Verified, not masked

Disinfection & Sanitization

Preventive and reactive disinfection for homes, offices, schools, clinics and public-facing facilities.

Whether you are responding to an exposure or protecting a facility ahead of time, the work has to be systematic. We map high-touch surfaces, apply EPA-registered disinfectants with the correct dwell time, and give you a record of what was treated and when — useful for staff, tenants and compliance alike.

  • High-touch surface mapping
  • EPA-registered products
  • Correct dwell-time application
  • Treatment documentation
