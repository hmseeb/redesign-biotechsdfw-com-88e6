Careers

Work that is difficult, and genuinely matters.

Biohazard remediation is not for everyone. For the right person it is steady, skilled work with a clear purpose: taking something unbearable off someone else's shoulders. If that appeals to you, we would like to hear from you.

What the role involves

Remediation technician work, based in Dallas or Fort Worth

Technicians travel to residential, commercial and municipal properties across the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex to contain, decontaminate and restore affected areas. The day involves protective equipment, containment setup, material removal, disinfection, odor treatment, regulated waste handling and clear communication with whoever is on site.

Previous remediation, restoration, hazmat, healthcare, construction or trades experience is useful, but attitude and reliability matter more. Training is provided for the protocols and equipment we use.

We are a small team covering a large metroplex from two offices, which means on-call rotation and a genuine willingness to respond when a call comes in at an inconvenient hour.

What we look for

  • A strong stomach and a steady temperament. This work is confronting. The people who do it well are calm, unflappable and able to leave the day behind.
  • Genuine respect for people in crisis. You will be in homes on the worst day a family has had. How you speak and behave matters enormously.
  • Reliability, especially after hours. We answer 24/7, which only works if people turn up when they say they will — nights, weekends and holidays included.
  • Attention to the parts nobody sees. The job is finished when the contamination is gone, not when the room looks clean.
  • Physical capability. Expect protective equipment, heat, heavy lifting, long days and tight spaces.
  • Absolute discretion. What you see on a job stays on the job. Confidentiality is non-negotiable.
